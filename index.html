What is project management?

At its heart, it’s just a way to turn an idea into reality — without dropping the ball.

Think of it like planning a trip: you have a destination (goal), a route (plan), a budget, and you check you’re on track. Project management gives you the same clarity for work, events, or any team effort.

  • Simple = clear goal + small tasks + regular check-ins
  • You don’t need complex software — just a bit of structure.

"Start small, think big"

The simple 5‑step framework

Follow these stages whether you’re planning a birthday party or a new website.

1. Initiate

Define the goal, who’s involved, and what ‘done’ looks like. One sentence is enough.

2. Plan

Break the goal into tiny steps, decide who does what, and set a rough timeline.

3. Execute

Start doing the tasks — keep communicating and checking things off.

4. Monitor

Quickly review progress, adjust if something changes, and solve small problems.

5. Close

Celebrate, collect lessons learned, and archive your notes for next time.

Tip: you can move back and forth — it’s a guide, not a prison.
